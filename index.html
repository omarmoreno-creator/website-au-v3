Frequently asked questions

Is Atlantis University accredited?

Yes. Atlantis University is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) and authorized by the Florida Department of Education, Commission for Independent Education.

Where is the campus located?

The main campus is in Miami, in the Miami metropolitan area, at 1011 Sunnybrook Road, Miami, FL 33136.

What languages are courses taught in?

Most degree programs are taught in English. The Intensive English Program (IEP) and select bilingual offerings support students whose first language is Spanish or Portuguese.

Does Atlantis University accept international students?

Yes. The university is authorized to enroll non-immigrant students and issues Form I-20 for F-1 student visa applicants. Dedicated international advisors support students from Latin America and the Caribbean.

Are programs available online?

Several degree and certificate programs are available fully online or in a hybrid format. Specific modality is listed on each program page.

How do I apply?

Submit the online application, official transcripts, a government-issued ID, and (for international applicants) proof of English proficiency or IEP placement. Detailed steps are on the Admissions page.

What featured degree programs does Atlantis University offer?

Featured programs include the Master of Business Administration (MBA, 45 credits, $40,365), MS in Artificial Intelligence (30 credits, $26,910), MS in Healthcare Management (36 credits, $32,292), and MS in Education (36 credits, $32,292). Full catalog of 27 programs is on the Programs page.

How much does tuition cost?

Tuition varies by program and modality. Per-credit tuition, fees, and payment plans are listed on the Admissions page. Atlantis University also offers institutional scholarships and accepts approved federal and private financing.

How long does it take to complete a degree?

Bachelor's degrees typically take about 4 years full-time and the MBA takes about 18–24 months. Transfer credits and prior learning assessments can shorten time-to-degree. Certificates can be completed in under 12 months.

Can I transfer credits from another university?

Yes. Atlantis University accepts transfer credits from accredited U.S. and international institutions. Submit official transcripts during the application process for an evaluation by the Registrar's Office.
