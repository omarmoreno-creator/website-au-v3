Why choose an MBA in Miami?

If you are looking for an MBA in Miami for international students or working professionals, Atlantis University offers a flexible hybrid program with evening classes in Miami, bilingual support, and no GMAT requirement. Program overview

A practical MBA built around Miami's economy

The Atlantis University MBA is a 45-credit graduate business degree accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC). It is delivered in a hybrid format from our Miami campus, with evening classes that fit around a full-time job.

The curriculum combines core graduate business coursework — accounting, finance, marketing, operations, strategy, analytics, organizational behavior, and ethics — with concentration electives covering finance, leadership, entrepreneurship, and an International Business concentration. A capstone project applies what you learn to a real Miami-area organization. Students interested in technology can also pair the MBA with our Artificial Intelligence programs to build cross-disciplinary skills.

Faculty include practitioners with U.S. and Latin American industry experience. Cohorts include working professionals, recent bachelor's graduates, and international students on F-1 visas, creating a bilingual, cross-border learning environment specific to South Florida. Why study an MBA in Miami

The city is part of the curriculum

Miami is one of the most internationally connected business markets in the United States. Studying here gives MBA students direct access to multinational employers, cross-border deals, and a bilingual workforce.

U.S. gateway to Latin America

Miami handles the largest share of U.S. trade with Latin America and the Caribbean. MBA students gain direct exposure to multinational employers and cross-border deals.

Bilingual business economy

More than 70% of Miami-Dade residents speak a language other than English at home. Bilingual professionals are in high demand across finance, logistics, and consumer brands.

Growing startup ecosystem

Miami has become one of the fastest-growing tech and venture capital hubs in the United States, with strong activity in fintech, web3, healthtech, and logistics.

Tax-friendly environment

Florida has no state income tax, which is a meaningful advantage for graduates building businesses or growing their salary in the region.

International students

F-1 visa support, end to end

Atlantis University is SEVP-authorized and supports international applicants from first inquiry through graduation. The hybrid MBA satisfies F-1 in-person attendance requirements.

MBA in Miami — Frequently asked questions

Is the Atlantis University MBA accredited?

Yes. The MBA is accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) and Atlantis University is authorized by the Florida Department of Education's Commission for Independent Education (License No. 3355).

How long does the MBA in Miami take to complete?

Most students complete the 45-credit MBA in 24 months while working full-time. Students taking a heavier course load each term can finish faster.

Do I need to take the GMAT or GRE?

No. The Atlantis University MBA does not require GMAT or GRE scores. Admission is based on prior academic performance, professional experience, and a personal statement.

Can international students enroll in the MBA on an F-1 visa?

Yes. Atlantis University is SEVP-authorized to issue Form I-20 for F-1 student visa applicants. The MBA's hybrid format includes in-person evening sessions in Miami that satisfy F-1 attendance requirements.

Are classes offered in the evening?

Yes. Core MBA courses are scheduled in the evening on the Miami campus, combined with online coursework, so students can keep working full-time throughout the program.

Is the MBA bilingual?

Courses are taught in English, but faculty and student services include bilingual English/Spanish staff. This is especially helpful for international students from Latin America and the Caribbean during admissions and academic advising.

What kinds of jobs do MBA graduates pursue?

Common roles include Operations Manager, Financial Analyst, Director of International Business, Marketing Director, Management Consultant, and Entrepreneur. Many graduates target bilingual leadership roles in Miami's cross-border economy.

Can I transfer credits from another graduate program?

Atlantis University evaluates graduate transfer credit case-by-case. Eligible students may transfer in qualifying graduate-level coursework from regionally or nationally accredited institutions.

Is this MBA STEM-designated?

The Atlantis University MBA is a general management degree and is not currently STEM-designated. Students who need STEM-eligible coursework can combine the MBA with electives or certificates from our Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics programs in Miami.

How much does an MBA in Miami cost?

Total tuition for the 45-credit MBA at Atlantis University is $40,365 ($897 per credit hour). Additional one-time fees: $150 application fee and $500 graduation fee. Per-semester fees: $50 registration. Textbooks are approximately $100 to $200 per course. Institutional scholarships, federal financial aid (for eligible U.S. students), interest-free monthly payment plans, and employer reimbursement help make the program affordable.
