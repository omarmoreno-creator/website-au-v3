Degree programs in Miami, Florida.

Atlantis University offers ACCSC-accredited master's, bachelor's, associate, graduate certificate, and diploma programs across five schools — Business, Information Technology, Engineering, Health, and Education. Programs are delivered on our Miami campus, in hybrid format, and fully online.
