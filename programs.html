Academics

Degree programs in Miami, Florida.

Atlantis University offers ACCSC-accredited bachelor's degrees, master's degrees, and professional certificates across six schools — business, technology, health, engineering, education, and languages. Programs are delivered on our Miami campus in Miami, in hybrid format, and fully online.

Use the filters below to narrow programs by school, degree level (BS, MBA, Certificate, Diploma), and format (on-campus, hybrid, online). Each program page includes credentials, duration, career outcomes, tuition information, and answers to common questions from U.S. and international students.
