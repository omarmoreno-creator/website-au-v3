Admissions

How to apply.

A clear, five-step process. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis with start terms in January, May, and September.

Application steps

  1. 01
    Submit application

    Complete the online application form. There is no application fee.

  2. 02
    Send transcripts

    Provide official high school transcripts (and college transcripts if applicable).

  3. 03
    Verify identity

    Submit a government-issued ID. International applicants submit a passport copy.

  4. 04
    English proficiency

    International applicants demonstrate English proficiency or enroll in the Intensive English Program.

  5. 05
    Interview & enrollment

    Meet with an admissions advisor, finalize financial planning, and enroll.

Requirements

  • —Completed online application
  • —Official high school diploma or equivalent (GED, foreign secondary credential)
  • —Government-issued photo ID (passport for international applicants)
  • —English proficiency: TOEFL 61+ / IELTS 5.5+ / Duolingo 90+ — or IEP placement
  • —Personal statement (one page)

Frequently asked questions

Is there an application fee?

No. Atlantis University does not charge an application fee.

When are the application deadlines?

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. Programs start in January, May, and September.

Is financial aid available?

U.S. citizens and eligible non-citizens may apply for federal financial aid (Title IV). Institutional payment plans are available to all students.

Do you accept transfer credits?

Yes. Up to 75% of credits may be transferred for undergraduate programs, subject to evaluation by the registrar.

What if my English isn't strong enough?

International applicants may enroll directly in the Intensive English Program (IEP) and transition into a degree program upon reaching required proficiency.
