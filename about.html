About

A small, accredited Miami university with international reach.

Atlantis University was founded in 2010 in Doral, Florida, to make accredited U.S. higher education accessible to working professionals and international students from across the Americas.

Mission and accreditation

Mission

To deliver career-relevant, accredited education in a globally connected setting — with the flexibility working students require and the academic standards U.S. employers expect.

Accreditation

Accredited by the Accrediting Commission of Career Schools and Colleges (ACCSC) and authorized by the Florida Department of Education, Commission for Independent Education. SEVP-certified to enroll non-immigrant students.
