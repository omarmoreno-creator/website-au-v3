International

Built for students from across the Americas.

Miami is one of the most internationally connected cities in the United States. Atlantis University is SEVP-certified, issues Form I-20, and offers bilingual support for students from Latin America and the Caribbean.

International student support

F-1 student visa

We issue Form I-20 to accepted students. International advisors guide you through the SEVIS I-901 fee and the consular interview at the U.S. embassy.

English pathways

Start in the Intensive English Program (IEP) and transition into a degree program once you reach the required proficiency level. No separate application required.

Bilingual support

Admissions, advising, and student services are available in English and Spanish. Many staff members are also fluent in Portuguese and Haitian Creole.

International student questions

Can Atlantis University issue a Form I-20?

Yes. Atlantis University is SEVP-certified and issues Form I-20 for accepted students applying for an F-1 student visa.

What English level do I need?

Degree programs require TOEFL 61+, IELTS 5.5+, or Duolingo 90+. Students below this level may begin in the Intensive English Program (IEP) and transition into a degree program upon reaching the required level.

Do you offer support in Spanish?

Yes. Admissions and student services are available in English and Spanish. Many staff members also speak Portuguese.

Are there services for students from the Caribbean?

Yes. We have dedicated advisors for students from Haiti, the Dominican Republic, Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, and other Caribbean nations.

Can I work while studying?

F-1 students are generally allowed to work on-campus up to 20 hours per week. Off-campus work is restricted; an international advisor can explain CPT and OPT options.

How much does it cost to study?

Tuition starts at $385/credit (undergraduate) and $565/credit (graduate). Living costs in Miami typically range from $1,500–$2,500 per month.
